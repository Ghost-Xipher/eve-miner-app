Ship cost

What this fit costs to buy at the trade hub chosen on the Ore Values tab, from the cheapest sell order there (no fees when you buy from orders). Skills and the pilot are not counted.

Price

Live prices at the trade hub chosen on the Ore Values tab, after sales tax (and broker fee when listing), with your reprocessing skills and station there. Uses only the modules that can mine this ore (a crystal's bonus only on its own ores).

Rates include the expected bonus from critical successes (chance × extra yield). Not counted: Mining Foreman bursts and other fleet boosts, implants, boosters, overheating. Waste is ore lost from the rock, not from your hold.

Mining Planner

Finds the best systems with asteroid belts near you: which ores their security usually has, how far the nearest station with a reprocessing plant is, how busy and dangerous they were in the last hour, and an ISK per hour estimate that counts the trips to unload.

Where
Mining

Yield and hold come from the Yield Calculator (change the fit). Ore is valued the way set on the Ore Values tab (hub, selling, reprocessing).

Belt ores follow EVE University's ore table by empire, security and nullsec region (CCP doesn't publish them), so they are a guide; ore anomalies, moon mining and ice fields are not covered. Click a system to see which belt to warp to. Ship kills and jumps are EVE's figures for the last hour. Score = ISK/hour lowered 30% in lowsec, 50% in nullsec and 8% for every ship killed there in the last hour.

Settings

EVE sign-in
Every EVE account and character signs in through the same built-in EVE application, so there is nothing to set up: click Sign in and pick the character on EVE's page. To switch characters, sign out and sign in again. Signing in lets EVE Miner read your skills, wallet, location and ship (with its fitted modules and hold, from your asset list) and set the in-game autopilot route. It never flies your ship.

Sign-in works in the desktop app (npm start or the .exe). In a plain browser everything else works without it.
